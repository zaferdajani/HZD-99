How we work · طريقة العمل

Pick a figure, then an action. Everything is drawn in code, so say what to change in plain words — "bigger shield", "longer beard", "prouder stance" — and I redraw. The strip shows the action as separate frames; the turnaround shows the figure like a rotating model.
