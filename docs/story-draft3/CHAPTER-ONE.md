# CLAWBYTE — The Machine That Said No

## Chapter One · story and lettering rewrite · Draft 3

Forty story pages plus cover. Written after the owner's request for stronger
suspense, character, and manga storytelling. This is the replacement production
script; existing illustrated proofs do not automatically implement this version.
Only the exact quoted dialogue, sound effects and bold prop text listed under
**LETTER** appear on the page. Speaker labels and editorial notes are directions,
never lettering. The companion lettering manifest separates these explicitly.
HZD-99 does not speak human language. Her short sounds, eyes, ears, hands, and
choices carry her performance. Do not add explanatory captions or thought balloons.

The emotional question is whether the little machine put away for being old can
find a place among others without having to make herself indispensable. The action
question is whether she can reach the part of the guardian that is still refusing
an order. The first question is answered by a place to return to; the second by a
choice the lion has already tried, painfully, to make.

Keep the approved game bodies throughout. The gray shield-guard body supplies the
street painter; it has a marking-tool attachment, not a new humanoid silhouette.
Meadow quarry enemies use the actual mechanical wolf bodies, not retired drillers.
Mother uses her assembled radial game design. The Eye remains a connector and iris
until its separate body is established. Record new staging as game work, not as
proof that it already exists.

## Lettering and production rules for this draft

- HZD-99 has no human dialogue. Keep chirps outside conventional speech balloons.
- Warm rounded balloons for voluntary speech; squared mechanical treatment for
  the imposed command. Mother has a distinct musical treatment, not the command font.
- Preserve intentional silent panels. Do not fill unused space with lore captions.
- The oldest invoice and the unfilled wake date are visual props, not narrator labels.
- Each speaker's tail must be checked against the final drawing, especially
  off-panel Ratchet during demonstrations. Text accuracy alone is not enough.
- Read page turns as pairs: 3–4, 8–9, 9–10, 15–16, 19–20, 25–26, 28–29,
  31–32, 32–33 and 39–40. The second page must answer or sharpen the first.
- This chapter grants one forged sword, then the first guardian upgrade. Separate
  dual swords and the connector remain later progression; do not depict them here.
- Publication requires the final art, lettering, continuity and game-parity checks.
  A complete script is not a finished illustrated chapter or a shipped game.
