# Draft 3 editorial review and production changes

Owner feedback: the corrected artwork is much better, but the comic is not
telling an engaging story or creating suspense. The rewrite changes dramatic
structure and exact lettering; it is not an assertion that the old text was good.

## What was failing

- Too many scenes were transactions: arrive, receive an explanation, get an item,
  move on. HZD-99 could appear obedient to the next instruction rather than choosing.
- The early Eye speech explained the motive before its behavior created curiosity.
- Supporting-character dialogue carried too many rules and not enough fear,
  avoidance, conflicting desires, or meaningful silence.
- Draft 2 told the hero that CHIME renews the binding, then staged her first cut
  like a failed plan. That made an informed protagonist seem to forget the warning.
- Generated balloons sometimes gave speech to the wordless hero or pointed at
  the wrong speaker. Image consistency alone cannot catch storytelling failures.

## Canon check

Preserved: peaceful robot world before the hijack; innocent Mother; deliberate
sleep and receiver isolation, not immunity; Ratchet's inherited marble and his
own hidden battery; separate spare cell for Servo; paid pack before repair/Burst;
rounded raw marble before Ratchet's forging; one first sword; first Sage before
guardian climax; ordinary recoverable victims; CHIME reinforcement; heirloom
cracks but remains; guardian upgrade earned after rescue; later weapon modes not
smuggled into Chapter One. HZD-99 has no human dialogue.

The script uses game cast shapes. New actions and props still require runtime
mapping. In particular, the peaceful call-and-response, tactile door response,
receiver dialogue/staging, linked CHIME encounter, teamwork, and final train
scene are not certified implemented merely because they are scripted.

## Review limits

This is a self-review of a complete script. Structural checks can verify forty
page headings and a lettering section per page. They cannot establish suspense,
reader response, or artistic quality. Those require the actual drawn and lettered
sequence to be read at its intended pace and phone size. No external editor or
independent agent review is claimed.
