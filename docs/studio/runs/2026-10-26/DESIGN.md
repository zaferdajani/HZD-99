## Story and tutorial verdict

The runtime preserves the owner's mandatory chain:

1. the hero wakes because it missed the corrupted Song while asleep;
2. Ratchet's letter and unique battery lead to the repair board;
3. Ratchet explains the marble, gives the spare cell, and sends the player
   forward through pod, Volt Pack and Servo teaching;
4. Gravehorn guards the raw marble;
5. Ratchet forges the purifier before the first Sage can be freed;
6. the Sage rescue unlocks CHIME;
7. CHIME precedes NULLFANG;
8. purification, not killing, ends the lion fight;
9. B1 begins Chapter Two.

The fresh campaign proves that causal route. It does not prove every optional
cave is meaningful to a first-time player, Draft-3 literal acting, physical
touch, a controller, or the cinematic with video enabled.

## Design decision

the owner-visible gaps are still Gravehorn production quality, literal manhwa
acting, optional-loop proof, purification films/body acting, notification
density and physical-device validation.
