## Chapter One causal walkthrough

1. A short circuit wakes HZD-99. The hero avoided the corrupted song only by
   being offline, not through immunity. Forward play teaches movement, jump and
   interaction.
2. Ratchet is found inert. His letter identifies his own battery and the repair
   board. Installing battery, terminals, wires and bridge revives him.
3. Ratchet explains that his marble pendant slowed the infection and that he
   removed his battery. He gives a separate cell for Old Servo, directs the
   player to the nearby pod, funds the permanent Volt Pack and teaches healing,
   attack and Burst without unexplained backtracking.
4. Old Servo is visibly powered off, larger than the hero and grounded. The
   cell wakes him; he identifies himself, explains the cave route and offers a
   coil errand with a named reward.
5. The caves supply evidence, rest, shortcuts and the raw marble behind
   GRAVEHORN. Returning to Ratchet forges the first purifier sword.
6. The sword changes the verb from killing to cleansing. The player rescues the
   Meadow Sage, silences CHIME, defeats NULLFANG to a purification threshold,
   deliberately holds the rescue input and sees the lion restored gold.
7. B1 opens and the chapter-two teaser appears. Alpha remains an optional
   post-forge Lost Wardens rescue, never a mandatory gate.
