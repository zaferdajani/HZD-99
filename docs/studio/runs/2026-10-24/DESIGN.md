## Chapter One causal walkthrough

1. A short circuit wakes HZD-99, who missed the corrupted broadcast only because
   the hero was offline. The opening teaches motion, jump, claw, hold attack,
   interaction, healing and Burst through forward-space causes and consequences.
2. Ratchet is found inert. His letter identifies his unique hidden battery and
   the repair board. The player installs the cell/wires, revives him and learns
   why his marble pendant only slowed infection.
3. Ratchet gives a separate spare for Old Servo. The nearby forward monument,
   pod and shop teach save/recharge, purchase of the permanent Volt Pack and
   purpose-driven recovery/combat lessons. No tutorial step requires unexplained
   backtracking.
4. The quarry/cave route supplies evidence, refuge, shortcuts and finally
   GRAVEHORN's raw marble. Returning to Ratchet forges the first white purifier
   sword.
5. The sword changes the goal from killing to cleansing. The player rescues the
   Meadow Sage, hears CHIME and then reaches NULLFANG.
6. NULLFANG is reduced to the purification threshold, not killed. A deliberate
   hold gathers white energy, the sword extracts and neutralizes the virus, and
   the guardian returns golden and friendly before B1 opens.
7. Alpha remains an optional post-forge Lost Wardens story: offer, search,
   rescue, friendly pack and reward. It never gates the mandatory chapter.
