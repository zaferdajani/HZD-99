# Chapter one, played — the integrated build of 2026-10-09

A first-person walk from waking to the chapter's end, as the integrated build
plays it. Each beat says what I see, what I press, why, what changes and what
points me on. The last column of the evidence table says HOW each part was
verified, because they were not all verified the same way:

- **Played with real input**: a harness pressing the keyboard (desktop
  1280×720) or touching the screen (phone 844×390 landscape; in portrait the
  game asks to be rotated), from a fresh save.
- **Staged**: a harness sets the save to the moment just before the beat and
  then drives the game's own interaction code (doInteract, the strike, the
  rest pod). The beat is real; the walk to it was skipped.
- **Screenshot**: the frame was rendered from the shipped pages and looked at.

## 1. Waking — the bay (W2)

The opening film ends on the storage bay. The first manhwa panels play once
(p04–p05, the bay), and then a chip in the lower centre says MOVE with my real
key (→ on a keyboard, the stick on a pad, the left thumb pad on a phone) and
one marker on the floor ahead. I walk. The chip says OUT; I leave the bay. A
low obstacle stops me and the chip says JUMP with my jump key. Every lesson is
one control, one marker, and it does not appear until the thing it teaches is
on screen.

## 2. The city gate (A0)

The gate panels play once (p05). The gate opens. The **first thing past the
gate is Ratchet's booth door** — the chip says UP/ENTER over it. Nothing
attacks me on the way: the yard winch stands further east, jammed and calm.

## 3. Ratchet's booth (A0B) — the letter, the battery, the repair

Ratchet is slumped and dark. Interacting reads his **letter** first (paged,
typed out at my text speed; one press shows the page, a separate press turns
it). The letter says his battery is in the drawer. The **drawer** gives a card
with the battery's own picture (repair_battery.webp). Interacting with him
again opens the **repair board**: I seat the battery with the board's own
buttons. He wakes.

He then explains, in five short pages: the Mother's song was infected; his
marble slowed the virus; he pulled his own battery; I was asleep recharging
and missed the broadcast; there is **raw marble under the meadow**; and he can
forge a **white sword** from it. The Ratchet panels play here (p07, p09 —
captions are the game's current lines).

He hands me a **Spare Power Cell** — its card names Servo, and its picture is
a squat steel cell with a gold window, nothing like his brass battery. Then,
word for word: *"This pod saves your progress and recharges you. Use it before
you leave."* The pod is highlighted. I use it: **"✓ Progress saved ·
recharged"** in the centre of the screen. Only then does the next lesson
appear.

## 4. The Volt Pack

Ratchet pitches the **Volt Pack** first — what it does (heal and charged
attack), why I need it, what it costs (12 scrap) — and pays me 12 scrap for
the repair, with a card explaining scrap. I never have to farm. The counter
opens on **"Volt Pack · permanent"**, marked BUY THIS FIRST, every other row
locked. After I buy it, that row becomes "Volt Refill". The pack's card shows
my own controls for both verbs and a small demo, and hands me back to the
world.

## 5. Heal, strike, burst — the winch

Outside, the chip teaches HEAL (hold), then the strike against the yard winch.
Claws only scratch it; the chip teaches the **Volt Burst**, and the burst stops
it. The lessons cannot softlock: a mashed strike cannot break the winch before
the burst lesson.

## 6. The monument

On the same screen, east of the booth door and before the way out, the **Mind
Node monument** teaches the puzzle. Then the chip says GO, and the road to the
meadow opens. A one-line **chapter goal** sits at the top of the HUD from here
to the Data Conduits and never sits on a lesson chip.

## 7. The meadow and Old Servo (A1)

Servo stands switched off, bigger than me and on the floor, with a cue over
him: *Switched off · needs a Power Cell* — and, because I carry the cell,
**"E — wake Old Servo"**. He powers up visibly. In three short pages he says
who he is, that the hub to the east has a loose floor down to the marble, and
offers the **gantry-coil task (60 scrap + 10 IQ)**. Talking again gets a
different line; when the coil comes home he thanks me and his drum spins true.

The meadow's wolves (the new filmed pack) prowl, coil and bite; their eyes
burn **red**, and a thin incense smoke follows the eye while they move.

## 8. The hub's landmark (A2) and the way down

From the hub's west edge I can see a column of pale light rising out of a
cracked floor, marble dust glowing around it; a caption on approach, the
strike prompt up close. A down-strike breaks through. (The first sight of the
lion — the staged meeting — happens on this road; the staged body takes no
damage, so nothing frees him early.)

## 9. The quarry (A5 → CV1 → CV1B → CV2 → CV3)

The cave panels play once on entering. White chalk arrows — the quarrymen's
road — lead down. Each cave pays something of its own (table below). At the
end of CV3 the **marble** shines; claws glance off it, the Volt Burst frees
it. I carry the marble home.

## 10. The forge

Ratchet forges the **Purifier**. The forge panels play (p18). On the way back
out, the arrows have turned **sage-blue** and the CV3 marks now point at the
**maintenance passage** beside a dark, cut stump that still glows along its
cut line. Machines I knocked out earlier ask to be freed now that I carry the
sword.

## 11. The first Sage (GA1T → GA1D)

Through the maintenance tunnel, the Meadow Sage kneels in the song-lock. The
fight ends in **cleansing, not a kill**: a column of light, slow rings, dark
motes rising off the body, and **RESCUED** on a dark plate. She frees the
people in her chamber on the spot, and her network wakes freed on every load.
She tells me about **CHIME** — the bell that writes the command back. The bell's
ward falls. The Sage panels play (p25–p26).

## 12. CHIME (A8 → A9)

The way up from A8 had a ward over it until the Sage was free; now it is open.
CHIME is a guardian: its lens burns **purple** and smokes as it drifts. The
CHIME panels play when it is silenced (p20).

## 13. NULLFANG (A3 → A4)

At A3's east edge the lion's enclosure had a song ward — three sheets before
the forge, two before the Sage, one before the bell — that pushed me back at
any speed (walk, dash, air dash). With the bell silenced it is gone. NULLFANG's
eyes burn purple. The Purifier frees him: **"The song lets go. NULLFANG is free
— cleansed, not killed."** The freed lion walks beside me on a real walk cycle,
his joints and eyes washed **teal** — the violet of the infection is gone from
him.

## 14. To chapter two

Climbing above the camp after the lion, the **chapter-two teaser** plays once:
crops of the tunnel page and the conduit gate, hint lines revealed one by one,
and the title card *CHAPTER TWO — THE DATA CONDUITS*. Nothing in it says how
chapter two ends.

**The Alpha** (A2's depth door, after the blade) is optional throughout: its
reward is a friendly pack — every wolf turns cyan, sits, and never hunts again —
and the road A2 → A3 → A4 never passes through it.
