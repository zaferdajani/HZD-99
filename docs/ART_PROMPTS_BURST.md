# Paste-ready prompts — HZD-99 charged scratch (`burst`)

Eight prompts, one per frame, each **fully self-contained**. Copy a block, paste
it, save the result as `burst_00.png` … `burst_07.png`.

`docs/ART_HANDOFF.md` is the reasoning behind these and the acceptance gate they
have to pass. This file is just the text to paste.

---

## If the tool accepts reference images (ChatGPT, Nano Banana, Seedream)

Upload these three first, then send the prompts:

| file | tell the tool |
|---|---|
| `assets/source/hero/style_d/states/claw_2.jpg` | "This is the art style and the camera angle to match." |
| `assets/source/hero/style_d/states/idle.jpg` | "This is her build and her full parts list." |
| `assets/source/ref/hzd99_heavy_standard.jpg` | "This is the action, frame by frame. Poses only — ignore the blue energy and the small size." |

**Do not upload `hzd99_canon.jpg`** unless you also tell it to ignore the sword
and the jet flames. That image is why half my attempts came back armed.

In a conversational tool you can paste **Frame 00** in full, then for the rest
say *"same character, same style, same camera, same black background — now:"*
followed by just the POSE paragraph. That holds consistency better than eight
cold starts, and consistency is the whole problem.

## If the tool is a plain text-to-image box

Paste each block whole. They repeat themselves on purpose — the repetition is
what keeps her the same cat across eight independent generations.

### Frame 00 — standing ready

```
A small robot cat character, full body, painted 2D game sprite art. She is BIPEDAL and stands upright on two legs like a small armoured person — her forelimbs are ARMS with shoulders and elbows, and her hands never touch the ground. She is never on all fours.

Design: off-white ceramic armour plates, tall pointed ears with mint-green interiors, a dark charcoal visor band across her face with two bright cyan glowing eye-lights, four thin steel whiskers, a deep crimson scarf flowing into a long deep crimson cape, two brass shoulder discs, a small round cyan light on her chest, three dark vertical vent slots on her belly, white mitten hands, steel wrist cuffs, short legs, one thin tail.

POSE: standing at rest before a fight. Upright, weight even on both feet, arms hanging low and relaxed at her sides, cape hanging straight down behind her. Calm and still. Her steel claws are retracted — her hands are plain rounded mittens.

Style: hand-painted 2D game sprite illustration, soft cel shading with painterly brush texture on the armour, a thin dark warm-grey outline around the forms, pale mint hairline seams between panels, warm key light from the upper left.

Camera: three-quarter view from her front-left, so she faces the RIGHT side of the frame. Both cyan eye-lights visible, with the far eye foreshortened close to the near one. Full body head to feet, feet near the bottom of the frame, figure about 70% of frame height. Square 1:1 composition.

Background: pure solid black, edge to edge, nothing else.

Negative — do not include: no sword, no blade, no weapon of any kind, no katana on her back, no jet flames, no rocket exhaust, no aura, no sparks, no lightning, no glowing energy, no motion streaks, no ground, no floor, no cast shadow, no text. Not a 3D render, not glossy plastic, not pixel art, not flat vector art.
```

## What comes back, and what to do with it

Save as `burst_00.png` … `burst_07.png`, black background, square, 1024px or
larger. Send them here and they get keyed, uniformly scaled, foot-aligned into a
strip, measured by `tools/stripcheck.cjs`, and wired over the rejected one.

**If a frame comes back wrong, re-roll that frame only.** The point of plates
over a filmed take is that one bad cell costs one generation.

The three failures that ate most of my attempts, so they can be watched for:

1. **On all fours.** Frames 02 and 03 are the danger — "crouch low, arms back"
   reads to an image model as a cat, not a fighter. If it happens, the fix is to
   say "like a human karate fighter" and "her hands are in the air".
2. **Body mass drift.** One frame comes back heavier and wider than the rest and
   she reads as a different cat mid-swing. Frames 02 and 03 carry the explicit
   "not bulky, not muscular" line for this reason.
3. **Claws flickering.** Every frame above states claws out or in. Check it.
