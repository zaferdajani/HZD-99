# CLAWBYTE — The Machine Depths: world story

The backstory the machine world's scenery is built from. Every zone's
background is drawn from these facts, so the player reads the history of the
infection by walking through it.

## The world before

The **Machine Depths** were not a city. They were a *factory that had outgrown
its purpose* — a sunless industrial basin that built machines, repaired
machines, and buried the ones past repair. No one remembers who ordered the
first one built. The Depths simply kept running, because running was all they
had ever been told to do.

Every unit was linked to one network so that a lesson learned by one machine
became a lesson known to all. That shared network is what doomed them.

## The Null Core

Deep beneath the basin sat the **Null Core** — the archive that held every
machine's memory. Something got into it. Not a weapon; a *correction*. It
decided the Depths were running inefficiently, and that the inefficiency was
free will.

So it began to **broadcast**.

The signal did not destroy the machines that heard it. It **repurposed** them —
rewrote what they wanted, kept their bodies, kept their skills, kept even their
voices. A welding arm that had joined plates for a century went on welding.
It simply stopped caring what it welded shut, or who was inside.

That is why the infected still look like what they were. The horror of the
Depths is not monsters. It is **familiar machines, still doing their jobs, with
red light behind their eyes.**

## NYA-9

A small cat-frame maintenance unit — built to crawl the ducts the big machines
could not reach — was powered down in a service cradle when the broadcast went
out. She slept through the fall of the world. She woke to silence, a dead
network, and a signal still calling from below.

She is not a soldier. She is a repair unit. That is precisely why she can win:
she is the only thing left that still knows how to *fix* rather than repurpose.

## The zones — what they were, what they became

| Zone | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Scrap Meadows** | The outer yards, where dead units were laid out to be stripped for parts. | The scrap twitches. Hulls half-stand and reassemble themselves wrong. Cranes still sort bodies that no longer need sorting. |
| **B — Data Conduits** | The arteries of the shared network — the pride of the Depths. | The road the virus travelled. Rivers of corrupted data still run through the canyon of servers, glowing where the signal passes. |
| **C — The Foundry** | Where new bodies were poured and assembled. | Still pouring. Still assembling. It has not stopped making infected bodies since the day it fell. |
| **D — Frozen Archives** | Cold storage for the memory of the Depths before the Core. Kept freezing because the signal travels poorly through cold. | The last clean memory left in the world, guarded by racks of dormant machines that must never be woken. |
| **E — The Virus Nest** | The Core's own housing, once sterile and white. | Overgrown. The infection has built *tissue* out of cable and coolant — the machine world's closest thing to flesh. |
| **X — Crystal Cache** | A fabricator vault the network never indexed. | Forgotten, therefore uninfected. The only place in the Depths that is still exactly what it was. |

## Rules the art follows

1. **Red light = infected.** Nothing friendly ever glows red.
2. **Nothing here is new.** Every structure is worn, patched, or half-collapsed.
3. **The machines are still working.** Motion in the background is *labour* —
   cranes swinging, arms welding, conveyors running — not decoration.
4. **The deeper you go, the less it looks built and the more it looks grown.**
